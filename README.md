### Mauro Castillo, MD, MS, CSCS, RSCC, CPSS

Physician turned sports scientist. I spent three seasons as a strength and conditioning coach in the Texas Rangers minor league system and was named 2025 Carolina League Strength and Conditioning Coach of the Year. I then completed a performance fellowship with the Texas Legends (NBA G League).

I build the tools a performance department actually uses: small, tested, and honest about what they know.

**How I work**

- **Physician.** Assess first. Know the anatomy and physiology, then progress, regress, modify or hand off to the right department.
- **Strength coach.** Sport-specific physical preparation, weighing research evidence against what works on the field.
- **Sport scientist.** Collect the right data and communicate it so staff can make decisions. AI is a tool for that, never the decision maker.
- **Leader.** Clear roles and expectations, accountability, and ownership of every decision.
